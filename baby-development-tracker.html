A baby development tracker for everyday observations

A first reach, a new sound, a different sleep pattern: the details can be hard to remember when you are caring for a baby. Leap Journey brings daily notes, activities and memories together without requiring an account.

What to record

Keep entries specific to what you observed. You might note a new movement, interest in an object, a sound, or a change in feeding or sleep. A date and a short note are often more useful to look back on than a general label such as “good” or “bad.” You do not need to fill every field every day.

The app includes daily logging for sleep, feeding, mood, movement, language and caregiver notes. These are your observations, not a diagnosis or an automated assessment of your child's health.

Activities by developmental phase

Open Activities to browse ideas associated with each phase. Two activities per phase are free, so you can try the format before deciding whether the full activity journey is useful to your family. Follow each activity's guidance, supervise play, and choose an idea appropriate for your baby's current abilities.

Keep memories separate from pressure

Save the moments you want to remember alongside your notes. Age-related phases provide context, not a promise about when an individual baby will develop a skill. The app does not diagnose delays or replace professional developmental screening. Bring concerns to your child's healthcare professional rather than waiting for an app to flag them.

Free and Premium, clearly separated

Daily logs, the phase timeline, two activities per phase, memories and pregnancy mode are included free. Optional Premium adds the full activity journey, patterns from your logs and reports. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: $2.99 in the US, with your local price displayed before buying.

Where your records live

Your child profiles, notes and photos stay on your device. There is no cloud sync. Apple and RevenueCat process purchases and purchase-related analytics, not your child logs or photos. Read the privacy policy for details and take care to preserve any records you need before deleting the app.
